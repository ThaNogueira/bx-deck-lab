# Carrossel v3 — assets de IA

Ferramenta: ImageGen embutida. Foram selecionados 15 chibis compactos (cabeça grande, corpo curto, mãos vazias), com alpha preservado. Os arquivos usados no site estão em `public/assets/ranking/chibis-v3/`. Os desenhos altos e com Beyblades foram descartados da implementação.

## witch-wave

Arquivo: `public/assets/ranking/chibis-v3/witch-wave.png`

Prompt final:

Use case: stylized-concept. ONE SUPER-DEFORMED JAPANESE CHIBI sticker based on this reference witch girl. VERY IMPORTANT PROPORTIONS: total figure only 1.75 heads tall; the HUGE ROUND HEAD including hat takes 65% of total height, and the tiny stubby body with tiny short legs together takes remaining 35%. Wide compact rounded silhouette, NOT tall, NOT normal anime body, NO long legs, NO long torso. Recognizable black hair with pink/lime streaks, cheek triangles, green eyes, tall black witch hat with X band, green/black cape, yellow tie. Pose: happy tiny hands waving empty, tiny feet close together. EMPTY HANDS: absolutely no Beyblade, no spinning top, no objects, no accessories held in hands. Crisp polished kawaii cel-shaded sticker, thick clean linework, two tiny cyan sparkle stars, thin cyan rim light. Entire compact figure visible with transparent padding. TRUE transparent background, no scenery, text, watermark, labels or logos. Keep costume simplified to suit the extremely tiny body.
